# Evocation Plus

**Evocation Plus** is a gameplay expansion mod for **Pathfinder: Kingmaker** that introduces new archetypes, bloodlines, spells, and abilities designed to expand arcane spellcasting while integrating naturally with the base game.

The mod focuses on improving offensive spellcasting and expanding underdeveloped magical archetypes while remaining faithful to Pathfinder mechanics and progression.

---

# Features

## New Archetypes

### Evoker

A specialist in destructive magic who channels raw arcane power through elemental bloodlines. Evokers enhance offensive spellcasting and gain abilities that increase the power and effectiveness of their spells.

Evokers gain access to **five bloodlines**, each focused on a different style of magic:

- Fire Bloodline  
- Cold Bloodline  
- Electric Bloodline  
- Acid Bloodline  
- Arcane Bloodline (force-based and utility focused)

Each bloodline provides thematic abilities that enhance spell damage and magical effects.

---

### Necromancer

The **Necromancer** archetype expands the Necromancy school of magic with new spells and abilities designed to make the archetype a more complete and powerful playstyle.

The mod introduces additional necromantic abilities and direct damage spells that allow necromancers to wield destructive death magic while maintaining compatibility with the base game's mechanics.

# Installation

1. Install **Unity Mod Manager**
2. Download the latest release of Evocation Plus
3. Extract the mod folder into:

```
Pathfinder Kingmaker/Mods
```

4. Launch the game and enable the mod in **Unity Mod Manager**
